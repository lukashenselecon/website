Cycling trip: Lama Temple to Jingshan Park

  • When
    Sunday, September 27, 2026, starting at 1:00 pm. About four and a half hours, including a coffee break and the Red Building.
  • Start
    Yonghegong (Lama Temple), main gate
  • Finish
    Jingshan Park, with a view over the Forbidden City and Beijing
  • Distance
    About 11 km, at a relaxed pace
  • Bikes
    Shared bikes. Please have a working, verified bike app (for example Meituan or Hellobike) before the day.
  • Bring
    Your passport, for the Red Building security check, and a few yuan for the Jingshan ticket.

The pace is relaxed, so no need to be a fast cyclist. If you can, come early and explore the area around the Lama Temple first. I especially recommend a stroll down Wudaoying Hutong, just across the street.

The route

  • ① 13:00
    Lama Temple (Yonghegong)
    Meet at the main gate, bikes ready.
  • ② 13:15
    Wudaoying Hutong
    West through the hutong.
  • ③ 13:35
    Confucius Temple and Guozijian
    Short stop under the old archways.
  • ④ 14:00
    Drum and Bell Towers
    Along Gulou Dong Dajie.
  • ⑤ 14:10
    Houhai and Qianhai
    Yandai Xiejie, Silver Ingot Bridge, and the lake shore.
  • ⑥ 14:30
    Coffee at Voyage Coffee
    Di'anmen Dong Dajie 97, at the south end of Nanluoguxiang. Half an hour off the bikes.
  • ⑦ 15:15
    PKU Red Building
    Free entry, no booking needed, but bring your passport for the security check. Last admission 16:30.
  • ⑧ 16:30
    Jingshan Park
    2 yuan at the gate, then up to Wanchun Pavilion for the view.

Plans can shift on the day. If you can no longer come, or you are running late, let me know in the class WeChat group.
