Cycling trip: Lama Temple to Jingshan Park

  • When
    Saturday, September 26, 2026, starting at 1:00 pm. About 4 hours, including a coffee break.
  • Start
    Yonghegong (Lama Temple)
  • Finish
    Jingshan Park, with a view over the Forbidden City and Beijing
  • Bikes
    Shared bikes. Please have a working, verified bike app (for example Meituan or Hellobike) before the day.
  • Sign up by
    Wednesday, September 23, 23:59 Beijing time

The pace is relaxed, so no need to be a fast cyclist. If you can, come early and explore the area around the Lama Temple first. I especially recommend a stroll down Wudaoying Hutong, just across the street.
